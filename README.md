# 🤖 Cys Task

Cys Task is a simple macro recorder and player. You can use it to automate Roblox, clicker games, or any other repetitive task on your PC.

Just Download the .exe from the latest release and run it.

---

## ⚡ Its Capabilities

* **🎥 Record and Play:** Record your mouse clicks, movements, and typing. Save them to run later.
* **⏩ Speed Controls:** Run your macros up to 8x faster, or slow them down.
* **🖱️ Autoclicker:** Press **F9** to click super fast at your cursor (up to 60 clicks per second).
* **🎨 Nice Themes:** Change colors to match your style (Cyber, Sakura, Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, or Luxury).
* **📂 Playlists:** Run multiple macro files one after another with custom delays.
* **✏️ Draw on Screen:** Draw lines, boxes, or write text directly on your screen.
* **🔔 Discord Webhooks:** Watch your screen for a specific color, image, or text. The app can send a ping and screenshot to Discord when it finds them.

---

## 🎮 How to Record and Play

1. Press **F5** (or click **Rec**) to start recording.
2. Do whatever actions you want to repeat.
3. Press **F5** again to stop.
4. Press **F6** (or click **Play**) to loop your actions.
5. Save your macro so you can load it next time!

---

## ⌨️ Hotkeys

Use these keys at any time to control the app:

* **F5:** Record / Stop Recording
* **F6:** Play / Stop Macro
* **F7:** Pause / Resume
* **F8:** Stop everything instantly
* **F9:** Autoclicker On / Off
* **F10:** Close the program

*(You can change these keys inside the Settings tab.)*
